# Soc Ops — Social Bingo

A Social Bingo game for in-person mixers and icebreakers. Players mingle to find people who match fun prompts on their bingo card and mark them off — first to get **5 in a row** wins!

## Features

- 🎲 **Randomized 5×5 bingo board** — each player gets a unique card drawn from a pool of social prompts
- ⭐ **Free space** — center square is pre-marked for everyone
- 🏆 **Bingo detection** — rows, columns, and diagonals all count
- 💾 **Persistent game state** — progress is saved to `localStorage` so refreshing the page won't lose your card
- 📱 **Responsive design** — works on phones, tablets, and desktops
- ⚡ **Fast & offline-capable** — built with Vite; no backend required

## How to Play

1. Open the app and press **Start Game** to receive your randomized bingo card.
2. Mingle with other people at the event.
3. When you find someone who matches a square (e.g. *"has a pet"*), tap or click that square to mark it.
4. The center square is a **FREE SPACE** — it's marked for everyone automatically.
5. Get **5 in a row** (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) and shout **BINGO!**
6. Press **New Game** at any time to reset and start fresh.

## Customizing the Prompts

Edit [`src/data/questions.ts`](src/data/questions.ts) to swap in your own prompts. The file exports an array of strings — just replace or add entries:

```ts
export const questions: string[] = [
  "bikes to work",
  "has lived in another country",
  // ... add your own prompts here
];
```

The board is generated by randomly shuffling 24 questions from this list (the 25th square is always the free space).
